# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-02-14

**Overview**
This app is provided and maintained by an independent developer. We value your privacy and data security. This app is a local schedule and to-do management tool. It does not provide an account system and does not require registration or login.

**Information We Collect**
This app does not actively collect, upload, or share your personal information.
Content you enter in the app is stored locally on your device, including but not limited to: course name, time, location, week pattern (odd/even weeks), notes, to-do items, reminder time, semester and period configuration, etc.

**How We Use Information**
Your local data is used only to provide app features: schedule display, conflict marking, to-do management, reminder notifications, widgets, Live Activity (Dynamic Island), and Siri/App Intents features.

**Data Storage and Transmission**
- Data is stored locally on your device.
- This app does not operate a server and does not upload your content.
- If you enable system-level backup or device migration, data may be handled by the system as part of those processes (determined by system mechanisms).

**Data Sharing**
We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal data to any third party, except as required by laws or by judicial authorities in accordance with law.

**Permissions**
- Notifications: used for local reminders for courses and to-dos.
- Siri and Search (App Intents): used to open Schedule/Today, query to-dos, or control Live Activity.
- Widgets and Live Activities: used to show today's schedule/to-dos and semester progress on the Home Screen and Lock Screen.
These permissions are used only for the corresponding features and do not cause your data to be uploaded or shared.

**Data Deletion**
You can delete courses, to-dos, semesters, and other items in the app to remove data. Uninstalling the app will delete local data on your device.

**Children's Privacy**
This app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

**Policy Updates**
We may update this policy when features change or as required by regulations, and we will notify you in the app or through relevant channels to review the latest version.
